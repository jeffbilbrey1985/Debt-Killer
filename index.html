Kill Mode Rules

This planner is designed to stop you from getting cute with the numbers.

  • The top row of the table is the account the planner wants you to kill next.
  • Your monthly attack payment snowballs automatically as debts get wiped out — paid-off minimums become fuel for the next account instead of disappearing into lifestyle creep.
  • Switch strategies if needed: Avalanche saves the most interest, Snowball gives you faster psychological wins.
  • Everything you type is auto-saved to your browser. Export a JSON backup any time you make a major change so you can restore it on any device.
  • Edit any field inline — name, type, balance, APR, minimum. No backend lock.
