Kill Mode Rules

This planner is designed to stop you from getting cute with the numbers.

  • The top row of the table is the account the planner wants you to kill next.
  • Click the ▸ chevron on any row to open its payment ledger, set credit limit, and see/edit your payment history.
  • When you log a payment, the balance drops in real time — the cell flashes green so you actually see it move.
  • Your monthly attack snowballs automatically as debts get wiped out — paid-off minimums become fuel for the next account.
  • Switch strategies: Avalanche saves the most interest, Snowball gives you faster psychological wins.
  • Everything auto-saves to your browser. Click ⬇ Export after any big change to keep a JSON backup.
