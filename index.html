Kill Mode Rules

This planner is designed to stop you from getting cute with the numbers.

  • APR is locked on the main page so the urgency ranking stays honest.
  • Your monthly attack payment snowballs automatically as debts get wiped out.
  • The top row is the account the planner wants you to kill next.
  • Paid-off minimum payments become fuel for the next account instead of disappearing into lifestyle creep.
  • Use backend edit mode only for true structural changes like a car trade or loan replacement.
